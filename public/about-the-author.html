Meet Tibi J. Roman

About the Author

Tibi J. Roman is the author of two books, Ambivalent Crossroads and The DNA of Executive Protection Site Security, each offering a distinctly different story. His writing draws on a lifetime of personal experiences, more than three decades of policing, and the knowledge and lessons gained throughout his life and career.

Ambivalent Crossroads (November 2027)

Ambivalent Crossroads is Roman’s latest book, a memoir based on true events and personal experiences. The narrative begins with his formative years before joining the Royal Canadian Mounted Police (RCMP) and continues through his 44-year association with the Force, including more than 35 years of police service.

His RCMP career began as a cadet at the RCMP Academy, commonly known as “Depot” Division, and encompassed a wide range of postings in both uniformed and plainclothes policing.

Roman served in general duty policing on three separate occasions: as a constable at Surrey Detachment (1979–1982) and Langley Detachment (1987–1991), and later as a non-commissioned officer (NCO) at Surrey Detachment (1997–1999).

In addition to frontline policing, Roman served in numerous specialized plainclothes units, including Immigration and Passport, Border Integrity, the Integrated Municipal and Provincial Auto Crime Team, VIP Security and Protective Services, the Premier’s Protection Detail, the 2010 Winter Olympics Integrated Security Unit, the Vancouver Integrated Intelligence Unit (Terrorist and Extremist Groups), and Departmental Security Risk Management.

Roman retired from the RCMP at the rank of Staff Sergeant, serving in a plainclothes capacity at the conclusion of his career.

The DNA of Executive Protection Site Security

The DNA of Executive Protection Site Security, Roman’s first book, draws upon his extensive experience and specialized knowledge in VIP security / Protective Policing.

During his RCMP career, Roman was designated a Protective Services/VIP Security Subject Matter Expert by Canada's national police force. This designation recognized his expertise and qualified him to assess the competencies of officers seeking promotional opportunities within Protective Services.

Roman’s involvement in VIP security began with temporary assignments early in his policing career and eventually evolved into full-time executive protection responsibilities. Following his retirement from the RCMP, he continued applying his expertise as a consultant and executive protection professional in the private sector.

Throughout his law enforcement career in British Columbia, Roman held numerous specialized VIP and protective policing positions, including:

  • Protective Services Unit Commander
  • Protective Services Operations NCO
  • Non-Commissioned Officer in Charge of the Premier’s Protection Detail
  • VIP Security Team Leader and VIP Security Section Team Member
  • Internationally Protected Person (IPP)/VIP Security Planner for the 2010 Winter Olympics
  • IPP/VIP Site Commander for the Vancouver 2010 Winter Olympics Opening Ceremony
  • Facilitator for the VIP File Coordination and Site Commanders Courses in British Columbia, Yukon, and the Northwest Territories
  • Syndicate Leader for the National VIP Security Course (Vancouver)

Roman’s executive protection experience includes providing security for a diverse range of individuals, including private citizens, consuls general, ambassadors, royalty, religious leaders, members of the judiciary, provincial premiers, Members of Parliament, Cabinet ministers, foreign heads of state and government, and other internationally protected persons.

His assignments also included security responsibilities involving Canadian Prime Ministers Pierre Elliott Trudeau, John Turner, Brian Mulroney, Kim Campbell, Jean Chrétien, Paul Martin, and Stephen Harper.

In his book, Roman shares the principles, planning considerations, and practical knowledge gained from years of providing protective security in demanding, often complex environments.

Author's Comments

Thank you for taking the time to visit my website and for your interest in my books.

Ambivalent Crossroads

The title Ambivalent Crossroads was suggested by my brother, Steve, after I shared several experiences and stories from my life and policing career. The more I reflected on his suggestion, the more I recognized how aptly those two words captured the underlying themes of my memoir.

Throughout my life, both before and during my RCMP career, I encountered situations marked by ambivalence, including conflicting emotions, uncertainty, and hesitation when faced with adversity.

In policing, these moments can carry particularly serious consequences. Decisions are often made under pressure, sometimes with limited information and little time for reflection. A single misjudgment can have profound, even life-altering consequences.

Ambivalent Crossroads is a collection of true stories about experiences that shaped my life, often in ways I never anticipated.

Some moments pass and are forgotten.
These are the moments that never did!

The DNA of Executive Protection Site Security

Executive protection is a unique, challenging, and rewarding profession. Yet when people think of VIP security, they often envision bodyguards, motorcades, and luxury vehicles. One of the most critical elements of effective executive protection is frequently overlooked: site security.

Consider this: whether a VIP is arriving at a location, departing from one, attending an event, occupying a residence, or travelling through an area, their safety is directly influenced by the security of those environments.

In essence, executive protection revolves around sites. Yet site security does not always receive the attention or recognition it deserves. Too often, its importance becomes apparent only after a serious security incident. The assassination attempt on Donald J. Trump during a campaign rally in Butler, Pennsylvania, on July 13, 2024, serves as a stark reminder of the importance of comprehensive protective security planning.

I wrote The DNA of Executive Protection Site Security to emphasize a fundamental principle: site security is not merely one component of executive protection; it is the DNA and foundation upon which effective protective operations are built.

Drawing on my experience in policing and VIP protection, the book explores the knowledge, principles, and practical considerations essential to this often-underestimated responsibility.

My goal is to encourage those working in executive protection to look beyond traditional close protection duties and adopt a more analytical, strategic, and comprehensive approach to protective security.

Ultimately, effective executive protection begins with site security.

For more information about executive protection training, including site security, accreditation, accommodation security, site vulnerability assessments, and security file coordination, please contact me through my website.
